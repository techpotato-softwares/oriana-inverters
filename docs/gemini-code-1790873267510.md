# TASK: Build a High-Impact, Modern Sustainability Page for a Solar Hardware Manufacturer

## 1. Project Context & Objectives
We are an emerging solar electronics & manufacturing brand in India. We need a modern, clean, authentic, and impactful Sustainability page (`/sustainability`).
Rather than corporate boilerplate or multi-year legacy ESG audits, this page must focus on pragmatic action: "Sustainability Built-In, From Day One."

Target Framework: Next.js (App Router or Pages Router, TypeScript preferred), Tailwind CSS, Lucide React (for icons), and optionally Framer Motion for subtle scroll animations.

---

## 2. Design System & Aesthetics
- **Style:** Clean, minimalist industrial tech aesthetic. Crisp typography, generous whitespace, subtle border dividers, soft ambient gradients (slate/neutral backgrounds with muted emerald, forest green, or warm solar accents).
- **Tone:** Honest, engineering-driven, forward-looking, and authentic to India's clean energy landscape. No greenwashing.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully mobile-first, fluid responsive design from mobile screens up to 2K monitors.

---

## 3. Page Structure & Component Breakdown

Create the page at `app/sustainability/page.tsx` (or `pages/sustainability.tsx`) composed of the following sections:

### Section A: Hero Section
- **Badge/Pill:** "Our Commitment • Clean Energy from Day One"
- **Headline (H1):** "Powering India’s Clean Energy Transition from the Ground Up."
- **Sub-headline:** "We don’t just manufacture solar hardware; we engineer every unit with a commitment to product longevity, responsible manufacturing, and circular lifecycles."
- **Key Metric Bar / Quick Stats (3 cards):**
  1. `97%+ Peak Efficiency` — Minimizing conversion losses across the grid.
  2. `100% Recyclable Packaging Goal` — Phasing out single-use expanded plastics.
  3. `Make in India` — Localized component sourcing reducing long-haul carbon miles.

---

### Section B: The 4 Core Sustainability Pillars
Present these in a responsive 2x2 grid or interactive card layout with clean Lucide icons:

1. **Design for Longevity & High Efficiency**
   - *Icon:* `Cpu` or `ShieldCheck`
   - *Headline:* Built to Outlast, Not to Be Replaced
   - *Description:* The greenest hardware is the one that stays out of landfills for decades. Our systems feature high-grade thermal engineering, industrial-grade silicon, and repairable component architecture that maximizes uptime and energy yield across India's harsh climate conditions.

2. **Conscious & Responsible Manufacturing**
   - *Icon:* `Factory` or `Boxes`
   - *Headline:* Cleaner Practices on the Shop Floor
   - *Description:* Clean energy hardware should not be born from dirty manufacturing. We enforce strict lead-free soldering, RoHS-compliant electronics, water-conscious assembly practices, and corrugated honeycomb cushioning over single-use Styrofoam.

3. **Empowering Local Communities & Supply Chains**
   - *Icon:* `Users` or `MapPin`
   - *Headline:* Made in India, Building Resilience
   - *Description:* Sourcing components locally doesn't just strengthen domestic manufacturing—it dramatically slashes international freight emissions. We invest in local engineering talent, transparent workplace safety standards, and regional technician upskilling.

4. **Product Circularity & E-Waste Stewardship**
   - *Icon:* `Recycle` or `RefreshCw`
   - *Headline:* Life-Cycle Responsibility Beyond the Sale
   - *Description:* Adhering to India's latest E-Waste (Management) guidelines, our enclosures and modular components are designed for straightforward disassembly, material recovery (aluminum, copper, and PCB silicon), and verified recycling partnerships.

---

### Section C: Interactive Carbon Displacement Estimator (Client Component)
Include an interactive slider widget (`"Sustainability in Numbers"`):
- **Slider Input:** Solar Capacity Installed (Range: 1 kW to 100 kW, Default: 5 kW).
- **Dynamic Output Cards:**
  - Annual Clean Units Generated (~1,450 kWh per kW/yr).
  - Annual Carbon Offset (~1.2 tonnes of CO₂ displaced per kW/yr based on Indian grid averages).
  - Equivalent Trees Planted (~15 trees per kW equivalent).
- Add a brief disclaimer note: *"Estimates calculated using Central Electricity Authority (CEA) average grid emission factors."*

- `Phase 3 (Long-term)`: Certified closed-loop takeback & refurbishment program for field units across India.

## 4. Implementation Guidelines
- **Component Cleanliness:** Break down into modular subcomponents where appropriate (`PillarsGrid.tsx`, `ImpactCalculator.tsx`, `CommitmentsSection.tsx`).
- **Styling:** Use modern Tailwind utilities: `bg-slate-50 dark:bg-zinc-950`, `text-slate-900 dark:text-zinc-100`, subtle borders `border-slate-200 dark:border-zinc-800`, rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`), and elegant card hover states (`hover:shadow-lg transition-all`).
- **Self-Contained:** Ensure no missing CSS classes, provide clean fallback props, and use `"use client"` directive on interactive components like the calculator slider.
